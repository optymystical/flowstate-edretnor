## How it runs
Ten launches, one at a time; the party as a whole must destroy each one. On one launch, the Festival Lantern, they must protect it instead. Top contestants score 8–9; **9 or more** takes first place outright.

- **Static dodge value:** each projectile has a fixed value instead of a die. An attack hits on a roll ≥ that value and crits on a roll ≥ 2× the value. A d15 can't crit a value of 8 or more.
- **No modifiers:** nothing can modify the value, and the projectile makes no defense roll.
- **Flight time:** one turn in the air. Two exceptions: the Heartstone stays up 3 turns, and Snapshot can only be hit with reactions.
- **Pacing:** the round runs as active combat. A projectile launches at the top of a round, the following round is an off-turn, and the cycle repeats. Energy follows combat rules and doesn't fully refill, so it becomes the budget. The off-turn is for recovering energy, retrieving thrown weapons and repositioning.
- **Catching** a projectile mid-flight takes Force of about 3× its health.
- **Summons count as living,** so Death's Wither and Slashing's +4 work on Wardwing.
- **The field:** projectiles fly through the lane 20–30 ft out from the contestant line (marked on the scene), so Area attacks and Blast weapons can reach them.

## The ten launches
| # | Projectile | Value | Health | Gimmick |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Fieldstone** (clay/stone disc) | 4 | 80 | Teaches the baseline. |
| 2 | **Hailshot** (10 pebbles, fired together) | 4 | 12 each | **All 10** must be destroyed. Mental Area attacks (radius, cone, line), Magic Area spells and Blast weapons can reach the lane. |
| 3 | **Ironheart** (iron ball, slow) | 2 | 200 | The damage showcase: Cleave, Striker's Rend, Heave, Acid's Melt, Destruction's Corrode. |
| 4 | **Skyglass** (tiny glass sphere) | 12 | 25 | About two hits, and no crits are possible. Rewards Advantage, Chant rerolls, Exact (which damages on a miss) and attack volume. |
| 5 | **Wardwing** (a small summoned creature; living) | 5 | 25 | **Ward 15**, which resets at the start of each party member's turn. **Flit** reaction cancels the first hit each party member's turn. Bait it with cheap or extra attacks (Fast, Solitary), stack reactions into one member's turn, Pierce, dispel it (it's a Spell), Wither or Slashing. |
| 6 | **Snapshot** | 6 | 35 | Launched mid-round, so it can only be hit with reactions (React, Burst, Shield Toss with RP, Lie in Wait). |
| 7 | **Veiled Wisp** (veiled by Illusion, humming) | 6 | 60 | Needs a Spot check (Moderate 10) to find; once spotted, Hearing gives its location. Unspotted attackers have Disadvantage, or Area-attack a guessed point. The veil is a Spell (Arcanomancy, Light's Dispersion). |
| 8 | **Festival Lantern** (protect) | 2 (for allies targeting it) | 45 | Drifts for 2 rounds while breakers fire **6 shots**. Each shot has value 6, health 5, and deals 3d10 (~16), so the lantern survives two hits. Stop shots by shooting them down with reactions, or protect the lantern: Shield, Accretion, Block, Redirect, Dampen, Mend, or catch it with ~135 Force. |
| 9 | **Geode** (stone core in a steel shell) | 6 | Core 40; shell 100 | The shell's Limit is 20 vs Physical and ~7 vs anything else, and only the core counts. Punching through with non-Physical damage, Pierce or Bash beats breaking the shell first. |
| 10 | **Heartstone** (3 turns) | 3 | 350–375 | Each turn a rune shows which damage category works (Physical, Elemental or Magical); a Spot check at DC 10 reads it. Matching hits deal full damage; others are doubly Weakened (×0.25). All Magic damage counts as Magical. |

The order is yours; the Lantern is public, and the Scout results reveal the twists (roll them on **Round 1: projectile twists**).

## Expected results
Tuned for a party of four starting characters (45/15, d15 attacks, Power 3, about 8 attacks a turn).

| # | Projectile | A baseline party succeeds |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fieldstone | 99% |
| 2 | Hailshot | 95% with one Area user making 2 Area attacks |
| 3 | Ironheart | 85%; about 93% with one object-damage booster |
| 4 | Skyglass | 68%; about 83% with one Advantage user |
| 5 | Wardwing | 75%; about 95% with extra-attack builds |
| 6 | Snapshot | 83% with 3–5 reactions |
| 7 | Veiled Wisp | 82% |
| 8 | Festival Lantern | 74% |
| 9 | Geode | 82% |
| 10 | Heartstone | 85% at 350 health; about 77% at 375 |

A baseline party lands around 8.2 of 10, and a party that uses its kit well lands 9–10. Under combat pacing, energy-hungry boosters show up less often, so real results run slightly lower.

**Prize pot:** 1st 2,500 shards; 2nd 1,500; 3rd 800; anyone else a 250 appearance fee. Rival scores: the favorites 8, the College apprentices 7, the Halflings 6, the Fire Walkers 6.
