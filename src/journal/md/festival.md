## Festival time
Each player picks 3 slots per day, individually.

| Slot | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Festival activity** | One of the activities below. At most 2 on Day 1 and 1 on Days 2–3 (4 total). Each can be won once per character. |
| **Shop** | Buy, sell or regrade gear. Edretnor is a city: Common items up to Grade 20, Uncommon in stock, Rare by commission. |
| **Scout** | Learn about the next round (see Scout results). |
| **Socialize** | Rival teams, Blackwell's meet-and-greet (Day 2), locals, the tavern. Foreshadowing beats and hook seeds land here. |

## Money
- **Starting purse:** 100 shards each.
- **Prize pot per round (party):** 1st 2,500; 2nd 1,500; 3rd 800; anyone else 250 appearance fee. Round 3 pays nothing (the attack cuts it short); the audience with the king is the prize.
- **Festival activity win:** 50 shards plus a Festival Token.
- **Reference prices (Market formula):**

  | Option | Cost |
  |---|---|
  | Regrade a Common item Grade 3 → 5 | 80 (light weapon, Icon, Foci, Shroud, Light armor); 120 (heavy weapon); 160 (Medium armor) |
  | New Uncommon Grade 5 item | 480 (light weapon, Icon, Foci, Shroud); 720 (heavy weapon); 960 (Medium armor) |
  | Affix | 200 Common; 400 Uncommon |

- The Round 3 lockers hold whatever gear the party owns by then.

## Festival activities
Three rounds against a festival regular rolling a d12; win 2 of 3. A starting character on a d15 wins about 2 times in 3. Stat contests roll the character's stat against the regular's, so a built-for-it character really is better.

**Festival Token:** a brass coin stamped with the activity's sigil. 3 uses; each use gives Advantage on that kind of check. Spent, it's a keepsake, so the tokens matter mostly during the tournament.

| Activity | Tests | Token |
|---|---|---|
| **The Lantern Hunt:** paper lanterns hidden through the old quarter | Perception (Spot) | Lantern Token |
| **The Climbing Spire:** a greased festival pole topped with ribbons | Athletics | Spire Token |
| **The Masquerade:** fool three judges about who's behind your mask | Deception | Mask Token |
| **The Crier's Stage:** win a crowd's vote with a speech or story | Persuasion | Crier's Token |
| **Hedge Maze Hide-and-Seek** | Stealth | Hedge Token |
| **The Bell Snatch:** first to grab a dropped bell | Initiative | Bell Token |
| **The Hall of Old Fears:** a haunted walk staged with Mental effects | Resist Fear (needs 30 on Con, Will or Build) | Ember Token |
| **The Drinking Horn:** each round is harder | Constitution check | Horn Token |
| **The Arm-Wrestling Table** | Strength check | Iron Token |
| **The Shooting Gallery:** static targets, a preview of Round 1 | Attack roll vs. a fixed dodge | Feather Token, plus 20 ammunition |
| **The Fortune-Teller's Tent:** catch her tells | Insight (Read Intent) | No token. She's hiding something: a hint for the crawl, and foreshadowing. |

## Day 1 evening: the draw and the tavern
- **The draw:** at dusk a herald reads the teams in the plaza, dozens of them. Each team gets a banner color and a sealed envelope with the public Round 1 format.
- **The tavern:** the festival inn is packed. The party sits together for the first time. Prompt each player: why did your character enter, and what would you ask the king for if you won? This plants the wish before anyone knows what it can do.
- **Rival drop-ins** (one or two per evening, Days 1–3):
  - The favorites size the party up and are dismissive.
  - The old-faith underdogs are refused service at the next table (a Mob justice preview).
  - The College apprentices are curious and friendly, one of them a little too friendly.

## Scout results
A Scout slot is a Persuasion, Deception or Insight check against an official's counter check (d12). Each success reveals the next entry for that round, in order. A failure reveals nothing.

**Before Round 1** (public: ten launches, one at a time, plus one launch you must protect instead of break):
1. "Not every target breaks to just any hit." Roll one twist on the d7 table below.
2. Roll two more twists (reroll repeats).
3. "The last one changes what hurts it, and the rune tells you how." The Heartstone's rune can be read (Spot, DC 10).

**Round 1 twists (d7)**, as an official would put it. The Fieldstone has no twist, the Lantern is already public, and the Heartstone is the third success, so they're not on the table.

| d7 | Projectile | What the party hears |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hailshot | "One launch is a fistful of pebbles fired together. Every last one has to break." (Area attacks shine.) |
| 2 | Ironheart | "There's a slow iron ball that takes a real beating. Bring your heaviest hitter." |
| 3 | Skyglass | "A glass bead no bigger than a thumbnail. Hard to hit at all; two clean hits shatter it." (Advantage and rerolls shine.) |
| 4 | Wardwing | "One of them is alive: a little summoned flier with its own ward, and it dodges the first hit each turn." (Bait it with cheap attacks.) |
| 5 | Snapshot | "One goes up mid-round, when nobody's ready. Only quick reactions catch it." |
| 6 | Veiled Wisp | "One is hidden by an illusion. You'll hear it hum before you see it." |
| 7 | Geode | "A steel shell around a stone core, and only the core counts. Punch through, don't smash." |

**Before Round 2** (public: a conveyor, protect one of your own, three waves, 100 yards):
1. The waves are locals, then staff, then the King's Guard. The Guard is the hard one.
2. The waves only go after the protected ally. Pick your toughest.
3. "A Royal Guard member closes the run." Only Supernatural protection stops him: Cold Iron or Silver, Shielding, Wards, Shrouds, temp HP. This is the window to buy for it.

**Before Round 3** (public: nothing; it's secret):
1. The theme is "Old Roots, New Branches."
2. It's underground, under a glass dome, and contestants are stripped of their gear at the door.
3. "Something alive is kept in the night garden." The specimen exists, so the party can plan for a fight with no gear.

## Foreshadowing beats (each noticed with a check)
- **Day 1:** "caravans" arriving in unusual numbers; a street preacher (a preview of the Recruiter); Fire Walker refugees harassed (a preview of Mob justice).
- **Day 2:** missing-person notices; guards quietly scrubbing black chalk circles.
- **Day 3:** Read Intent on the king's double shows something is off; an Elven official in the royal box too interested in the Royal Guard (the Sympathizer); a rival team member goes missing.
- **Day 4:** the Steward is nervous in the escape room.

## Day 3 evening: the play, "The Pilgrimage"
Paan's twenty-year pilgrimage and the sealing of the southern Rift, the old tradition staged with new crystal-lantern stagecraft.
- **Lore:** the Era of Claws, Paan and the Dusken curse in one scene.
- **The locals:** at their most likeable, the night before everything burns.
- **Rivals:** the Halfling crowd-pleasers perform the comic interlude.
- **Hook 10 seed:** a Dusken in the crowd heckles the ending with the Umbra cult's claim that "Umbra took him."

## The festival market (Shop slot)
Edretnor is a city, and the festival has drawn every merchant in the region. Prices use the Market formula with no festival markup; Barter's Haggle applies as normal. Regrading at a smith is done by the next morning.

**Always in stock (Common materials, any Grade up to 20):** every weapon and armor type, Common Icon Forms (Aegis, Sanctum, Veil, Warden), every Foci and Shroud type (they have no rarity; their affixes carry it), ammunition, and basic gear.

| Item (normal-work material) | Grade 3 | Grade 5 |
|---|---|---|
| Light weapon, Light armor, Icon, Foci, Shroud | 160 | 240 |
| Heavy weapon | 240 | 360 |
| Medium armor | 320 | 480 |
| Heavy armor | 480 | 720 |

Half-work materials (Softwood, Copper, Cloth, Hide, Titanium) cost half; double-work (Bone, Stone) cost double.

**The jeweler:** 1d3 Common affixes each day (d5: Quartz, Agate, Jasper, Obsidian, Hematite), 200 each.

**The Watch quartermaster:** once a party has the third Round 2 Scout result ("a Royal Guard member closes the run"), it can find surplus here. A Persuade (Moderate 10) buys one Cold Iron Heavy Defender shield (Grade 5, 720) and one set of Silver Light armor (Grade 5, 480). This guarantees that a party that scouted well has the answer to Blackwell for sale.

**Potions:** three tiers. The limit is still one potion per rest, shared between Health and Energy.

| Tier | Health potion | Energy potion | Price | At the apothecary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lesser | Restores half the health a rest would | Grants half your max energy | 200 | 1d4 of each per day |
| Regular | Restores what a rest would | Grants your max energy | 500 | 1d2 of each per day |
| Greater | Restores twice what a rest would | Grants twice your max energy | 1,200 | By commission, ready next morning |

All energy potions put their energy in a separate pool that lasts up to a minute or until used, so it can take you over your cap.

**Ammunition:** 20 for 20 shards.
